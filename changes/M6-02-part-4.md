- M6-02 part 4 (#601): ZOOM, PAN, the mouse wheel and real-time zoom and pan in a layout viewport made current (MSPACE) now change that viewport's own view, its scale on the sheet, rather than moving the sheet, as AutoCAD's do. Each is an undo step joined to the zooms and pans next to it, ZOOM Previous steps back through the viewport's own views, ZOOM nXP sets its scale, a display-locked viewport keeps its view, and the save writes the new view.
