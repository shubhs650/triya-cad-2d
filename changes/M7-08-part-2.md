- M7-08 part 2 (#PART2): the Diagnostics palette, on the palette rail and in Tools > Drawing Utilities. For the drawing on screen it shows entity counts by type, each canvas's frame times (last, mean and maximum of the last 60) and scene cache, whether the renderer and the command engine run in workers and how fast they answer, the last command's time, and the PDF page cache, read again every second. Nothing in it leaves the device.
