- M2-15 (#221): CTB/STB plot style tables are read and written (`parsePlotStyleTable`, `writePlotStyleTable`), `PlotResolver` gives what each object plots with (colour after grayscale and screening, lineweight, linetype, ends, joins, fills, pens) through a CTB, an STB or no table, and Triya bundles its own default, monochrome and grayscale tables in `assets/plotstyles`, also found under AutoCAD's names (monochrome.ctb, acad.ctb, …).
