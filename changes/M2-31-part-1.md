- M2-31 part 1 (#276): more ways to dimension. DIMLINEAR and DIMALIGNED measure a circle you pick; DIMANGULAR's Quadrant keeps the angle you chose whatever side the text goes, carrying the arc on to it; DIMCONTINUE and DIMBASELINE go on from ordinate and two-line angular dimensions; and new dimensions take the dimension settings a drawing holds apart from its current style as their overrides, as AutoCAD does.
