- M2-07 part 3 (#234): MTEXT columns and the editor's ruler. MTEXT's Columns option and the editor's Columns menu make static or dynamic columns (Column Settings sets their number, widths, gutter and height; Alt+Enter breaks the column), and dynamic columns count themselves as the text flows. The ruler above the editor sets each paragraph's first-line and left indents and its tab stops. Columns, indents, tabs and double-byte characters save to DXF and read back exactly.
