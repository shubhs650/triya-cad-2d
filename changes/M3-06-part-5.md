- M3-06 part 5 (#408): AutoCAD's temporary override keys (TEMPOVERRIDES): held at a prompt for a point, Shift turns ORTHO the other way, Shift+X POLAR, Shift+Q OTRACK, Shift+A OSNAP, Shift+D every snap and tracking off, Shift+S the running object snaps on, and Shift+E, Shift+M or Shift+C snap to Endpoint, Midpoint or Center only (each with its right-hand key too), listed and remappable in the Keyboard Shortcuts dialog.
