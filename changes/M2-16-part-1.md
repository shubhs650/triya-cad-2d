- M2-16 part 1 (#243): vector PDF writer. `packages/pdf` writes PDF 1.7 pages at their exact paper size with page labels and bookmarks, and plots render paths with their plot style: lineweights in paper millimetres, colour, monochrome and screening through the CTB/STB resolver, end and join styles, transparency, clipping and the 31 plot style linetypes (ISO types sized by the line width, adaptive adjustment). Stroke-font text plots as paths. A three-page test plot is checked with qpdf in CI and against golden images drawn by pdf.js.
