- M5-19 part 3 (#585): with LAYEREVAL 2, layers another drawing brings into this one (PASTECLIP, PASTEORIG, PASTEBLOCK, INSERT of a drawing, a layer added from DesignCenter) are unreconciled new layers, listed under Unreconciled New Layers until reconciled; layers you make yourself are not, and a pasted layer no longer carries the other drawing's unreconciled flag along. LAYEREVALCTL, a per-user setting (initially 1), turns the evaluation off at 0 and hides the Unreconciled New Layers node and Reconcile Layer while it is off. LAYERNOTIFY takes AutoCAD 2024's full range (0–63, up to 32 Insert) and is known in a drawing without it, initially 0.
