- M9-10 (#659): copies carry their constraints everywhere. ARRAY's separate items, grip Copy and PASTECLIP now give the copies the constraints among the objects copied, as COPY, MIRROR and ROTATE/SCALE's Copy already did, and dimensional constraints are copied too, under new names, as the new per-user PARAMETERCOPYMODE says (0 none, 1 values, 4 expressions). OOPS brings back the constraints ERASE removed, and a dimensional constraint dropped with its object leaves the expressions that named it their values.
