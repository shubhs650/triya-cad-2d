- M3-13 (#404): group follow-ups. A group selected whole shows one grip at its centre with its bounding box (GROUPDISPLAYMODE; "Group Bounding Box on/off" in Ctrl+K), and that grip moves the whole group. Copying a whole group with COPY, ARRAY, MIRROR, grip Copy or paste makes a new unnamed group of the copies. PICKSTYLE 2 selects an associative hatch's boundary with it.
