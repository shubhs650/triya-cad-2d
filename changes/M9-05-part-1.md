- M9-05 part 1 (#650): geometric constraints in the app. GEOMCONSTRAINT (GCON) and GCHORIZONTAL, GCVERTICAL, GCPERPENDICULAR, GCPARALLEL, GCTANGENT, GCCOINCIDENT, GCCONCENTRIC, GCCOLLINEAR, GCSYMMETRIC, GCEQUAL and GCFIX apply AutoCAD's constraints with its prompts, solved by PlaneGCS in the engine worker (loaded the first time a constraint is applied): the second object adjusts to the first, a constraint that would over-constrain the geometry is refused with the reason, and U takes a constraint back with what it moved. The new Parametric ribbon tab's Geometric panel holds the twelve, Smooth greyed out until spline constraints. Constraints last for the session: saving them comes with M9-04.
