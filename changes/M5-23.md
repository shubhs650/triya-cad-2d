The canvas hands its views to the render worker only with its frames, one at a time, so while a slow frame (raster images) was drawn the worker still tested picks under the view from before the zoom. Picks, nested picks, Shift+Space, the selection cycling badge, the command preview and the selection preview's pickbox now send the view their point was taken in with the request; a test holds the render worker's frames so the view lags every run.
