- M3-08 (#397): groups. GROUP makes named or unnamed groups, -GROUP lists, reorders, adds, removes, explodes, renames and makes them selectable, UNGROUP and GROUPEDIT work on a group picked by one of its members, and CLASSICGROUP opens the Object Grouping dialog with the drawing's named groups. With group selection on (PICKSTYLE, Ctrl+Shift+A) one pick takes the whole group. Groups save to DXF in ACAD_GROUP with their members linked both ways.
