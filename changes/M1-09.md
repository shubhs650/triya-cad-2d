- M1-09 (#84): bundled Hershey stroke fonts. Text whose SHX font is missing now draws with the nearest of ten fonts converted from the original public-domain Hershey data (Roman Simplex for romans, simplex, txt and the ISO fonts; Duplex, Complex, Triplex, Italic, Script and Gothic for their AutoCAD namesakes), including Latin-1 accented letters, ° ± and a `%%c` diameter sign, and every substitution is recorded for the health report. LibreCAD fonts are not bundled: they are GPL (DEC-044).
