- M8-03 part 1 (#693): the Library palette. LIBRARY (LIB, or Insert > Library) lists the symbol library's flanges, nozzles, supports, weld and surface texture symbols and standard notes, found by every word searched; the item picked shows its options and a preview, and Insert attaches its block, drawn in the drawing's units, to the cursor at scale 1. Favorites and Recent keep items with their options on the device, drawn the same size in a drawing of other units.
