- M5-16 (#563): COPY, MIRROR and ARRAY of a clipped block reference keep its XCLIP, as AutoCAD's copies do: each copy gets a clip of its own (an inverted clip's inner boundary and an XCLIP OFF state too), filed in its own extension dictionary and saved where AutoCAD keeps it, and a mirrored copy's clip is mirrored with the block. An associative array keeps the clip on the source it takes into its block, and COPY's and ARRAY's Undo take a copy's clip back with the copy. BLOCK and EXPLODE, which copy through the same edit, keep nested clips too.
