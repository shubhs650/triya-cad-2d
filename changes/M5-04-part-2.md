- M5-04 part 2 (#509): xrefs can be bound, from the References palette's Bind… (the Bind Xrefs dialog: Bind or Insert) or with -XREF Bind (as BINDTYPE says). A bound xref is a block of the drawing, with the xrefs nested in it: Bind names its layers, styles and blocks `xref$0$name`, Insert gives them their own names and uses the drawing's where a name is taken. The saved DXF holds it whole and opens without the xref files; Bind is one undo step.
