- M1-05 (#118): Triya now saves through its own exporter. `exportDocument` starts from the retained acad-ts original and applies, by handle, only what differs from the original's mapping (changed fields of LINE, XLINE, RAY, CIRCLE, ARC, ELLIPSE, POINT, LWPOLYLINE, TEXT and MTEXT, deletions with their owned extension dictionaries, new entities under engine-allocated handles, `$HANDSEED`); anything it cannot write yet is refused with `UnsupportedChangeError` instead of being dropped. Unmapped objects are listed in `DocumentInit.passthrough` so the engine never reuses their handles. The round-trip suite now saves through the exporter and requires zero differences on all 118 readable corpus files.
