- M7-03 part 2 (#626): the palette stack floats, docks at the bottom and auto-hides. Drag it off its dock by its tab strip and it floats where it is dropped (its place and size kept); drop it at the drawing area's left, right or bottom edge and it docks there. The Palette options menu (⋮) docks it left, right or at the bottom, floats it, and turns on auto-hide, which collapses the stack to its tab strip until the pointer rests on it. F2 now shows the command history as the Command History palette of the stack.
