- M7-03 part 1 (#625): the palette stack. Palettes now open together as tabs of one stack beside the drawing area instead of one at a time: tabs reorder by dragging (or Ctrl+Shift+Page Up/Down), the stack docks right or left and is sized at its inner edge, and that layout comes back next session. Below 1366 px the stack slides over the drawing instead of narrowing it, and Esc slides it away.
