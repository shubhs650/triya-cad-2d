- M4-05 part 3 (#438): table cells are selected by a click inside them (Shift+click selects the cells up to another; a click on a table's lines still selects the table), and the right-click menu (AutoCAD's cell shortcut menu, also TABLECELL's options at the command line) inserts rows above or below and columns left or right, deletes rows and columns, merges cells (All, By Row, By Column) and unmerges them, or edits the cell's text. Each is one undo step, and the edited table saves and reads back.
