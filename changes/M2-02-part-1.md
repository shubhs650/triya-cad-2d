- M2-02 part 1 (#222): the command line. A docked command window under the layout tabs shows the last history lines and the prompt, with the prompt's options as clickable chips, completes commands as they are typed, recalls input with Up/Down and opens the whole history with F2; typing anywhere outside a field goes to it, and Enter or Space ends the input, as in AutoCAD. Each drawing's command engine now runs in its engine worker, its edits reaching every canvas of the drawing, and the canvas picks points for a command waiting at one, with a rubber band from the base point. LINE is the first real command (Undo, Close, Enter continuing from the last line, Esc keeping the segments drawn): `L ↵ 0,0 ↵ @100<45 ↵ ↵` draws the line.
