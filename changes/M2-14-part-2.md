- M2-14 part 2 (#242): copy and paste between open drawings. COPYCLIP, CUTCLIP and COPYBASE (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+Shift+C) put objects on one Clipboard every drawing shares; PASTECLIP, PASTEBLOCK and PASTEORIG (Ctrl+V, Ctrl+Shift+V) paste them with the layers, linetypes, styles and blocks they use, matched by name in the target, every new object above its `$HANDSEED`, dragged with the cursor. Every readable corpus file's model space pastes into an AC1032 drawing that saves and reads back whole. DRAWINGRECOVERY shows the recovery banner again. Hatch extents follow their arc edges' sweep; a save writes new blocks before the dimension styles that name them.
