- M8-06 (#688): the Compare palette. COMPARE (or View > Compare) picks an original and a revision, open drawings or files, and makes a compare drawing as a new tab: the revision with what it added in green, modified in amber and kept in grey, and the original's removed objects and previous versions in red. Its list of differences zooms to each, filters by status, layer and type hide the rest, and Export DXF... and Export PDF... save and plot it as shown.
