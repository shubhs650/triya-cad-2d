- M2-30 part 2 (#273): dimensions follow their style. Choosing another dimension style, a text override or a DIMVAR override in the Properties palette (Dim style, Text override, Arrow size, Ext line ext, Ext line offset, Text height, Text offset, Dim scale overall, Dim scale linear, Precision) redraws the dimension at once, as does any change to the dimension style it uses; and a dimension opened without its block is saved with one, so the file holds what Triya draws for it.
