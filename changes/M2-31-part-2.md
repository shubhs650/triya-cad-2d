- M2-31 part 2 (#278): a radius or diameter dimension placed past the end of the arc it measures now draws an arc extension line along the arc, as AutoCAD does, and keeps doing so after the dimension is moved by its grips, rotated, mirrored or copied. When you type a dimension's text in the MTEXT editor (the Mtext option or a double click), the editor shows the measurement itself, highlighted, where `<>` stands, so you see what you are adding to.
