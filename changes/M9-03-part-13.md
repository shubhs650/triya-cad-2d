- M9-03 part 13 (#PR): dimensional constraints in a layout's viewports. Annotational constraints show through every viewport that shows their objects, as dimensions in model space do, and plot with the layout, carried and clipped as the viewport's objects; dynamic ones show only through the viewport made current, where model space is worked in, and never plot. A viewport leaves out the constraints whose objects are all on layers frozen in it. Through the current viewport a constraint is picked, highlighted and shows its grips, as on the Model tab.
