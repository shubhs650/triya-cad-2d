- M2-10 part 3 (#239): dimensions regenerate when touched. STRETCH moves the definition points of a dimension inside its window and SCALE changes what it measures; the dimension engine then draws it afresh (new measurement, text recentred) and its `*D` block is rewritten, so the canvas, extents and the saved DXF all show the edited dimension. The STRETCH preview draws the dimension afresh as the cursor moves. M2-10 closes; leftovers are M2-29.
