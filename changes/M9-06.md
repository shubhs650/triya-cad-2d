- M9-06 (#654): modify commands keep geometric constraints in step. ERASE removes an erased object's constraints (U brings them back); TRIM, EXTEND, FILLET, CHAMFER, BREAK, EXPLODE and JOIN drop the constraints on what they change, saying how many; MOVE, ROTATE, SCALE, MIRROR, STRETCH and grip drags re-solve, so geometry joined to what moved follows it; an edit the constraints cannot take, such as rotating a horizontal line or moving a fixed one, is refused with the reason; and COPY, MIRROR and ROTATE/SCALE's Copy give the copies the constraints among the objects copied.
