- M9-09 (#656): constrained lines keep their length when an edit turns them. Dragging the end of one of a parallel pair turns the other about its start instead of shortening it, and the dragged line keeps its start; lines joined to a turned line ride along with their lengths kept, and ROTATE of one of a parallel pair turns the other the same way. Lines held only by joints still stretch, so a constrained rectangle resizes from a corner or side.
