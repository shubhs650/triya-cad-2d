- M2-17 part 4 (#261): the Plot dialog now opens on the whole page setup the drawing stores, in model space and layouts: the paper, orientation, plot area (including a saved window), Fit to paper or the scale, centring and the offset. Its new Apply to Layout button saves the dialog's settings into that page setup (one undo step, kept in the DXF), so the next PLOT, PREVIEW or PUBLISH opens on them.
