- M5-04 part 5 (#516): IMAGEATTACH (IAT, or Attach Image... in the References palette) attaches a BMP, JPEG, PNG, TIFF or GIF file: the Attach Image dialog picks the file (Browse...) or one of the drawing's images, shows its preview and, with Show Details, its resolution and size, and places it by insertion point, scale and rotation, typed in the dialog or given on screen. The image is sized as AutoCAD sizes it (by the file's resolution and the drawing's units, or the scale as its width), draws at once, saves to DXF with its definition, and is one undo step.
