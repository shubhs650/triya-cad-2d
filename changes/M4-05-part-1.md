- M4-05 part 1 (#431): the table engine and -TABLE. A new table's `*T` block is drawn from its cells and table style as AutoCAD's tables hold theirs (fills, cell texts, fitted block cells, grid lines joined where alike and broken at merges and hidden edges); it redraws 17 of the 28 corpus tables exactly and the rest for stated reasons. -TABLE inserts a table of the current style (AutoCAD's Standard where the drawing has none) with a title, header and data rows, and the nozzle-schedule table it makes saves as AC1032 DXF from every corpus drawing and reads back as drawn.
