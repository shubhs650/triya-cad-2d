- M3-12 part 1 (#402): the modify commands' options are system variables SETVAR reads and sets, as in AutoCAD: OFFSETERASE, OFFSETGAPTYPE, TRIMEXTENDMODE, EDGEMODE, PROJMODE, TRIMMODE and PEDITACCEPT kept per user in Settings, OFFSETDIST and CHAMMODE while the app runs; OFFSET, TRIM, EXTEND, FILLET, CHAMFER and PEDIT start from them and set them when an option changes. OFFSETGAPTYPE 1 fills the outside corners of an offset polyline with arcs and 2 with chamfers, and PEDITACCEPT 1 turns a line, arc or spline into a polyline without asking.
