- M5-22 (#586): a point typed right after a click now counts from the point clicked. A click at a point prompt was not counted as an answer the keys after it wait for, so `@20<30` typed while LINE's next prompt was still on its way went into Dynamic Input's fields for the prompt the click had answered, and the new segment was measured from the point before the click (seen on CI as a missed relative polar snap in `drafting-settings.spec.ts`). Clicks at point, distance and angle prompts are now numbered like typed input, so keys typed after them wait for the prompt they lead to, as AutoCAD buffers type-ahead; a test holds the engine's prompts after the click so the keys arrive first every run.
