- M2-11 (#241): dimension commands. DIMLINEAR (two origins or a picked line, arc or polyline segment; horizontal or vertical by where the dimension line goes, or Rotated), DIMALIGNED, DIMRADIUS, DIMDIAMETER, DIMANGULAR (two lines, an arc, a circle, or three points), DIMCONTINUE and DIMBASELINE, with their Mtext, Text and Angle options. The dimension follows the cursor while it is placed, is drawn by the dimension engine in the current dimension style and says "Dimension text = …". Dimensions now have grips (extension line origins, dimension line, text) and redraw when one is dragged; TEXTEDIT and a double click edit a dimension's text, `<>` standing for the measurement. Leftovers are M2-31.
