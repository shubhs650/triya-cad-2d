- M2-14 part 1 (#240): crash recovery and recent drawings. A drawing with unsaved changes is autosaved to this device (IndexedDB) within 5 s of its last edit, never to its file; after a crash, a killed tab or a window closed with unsaved changes, the next launch offers "Recover unsaved drawing?" with Recover and Discard, and the recovered drawing opens with its changes and the unsaved dot. Drawings opened or saved through a file handle are listed as recent drawings on the start screen and in the Ctrl+K palette.
