- M3-10 part 2 (#401): AutoCAD's keyboard shortcuts, remappable. The F-keys and Ctrl keys work as in AutoCAD (Ctrl+L Ortho, Ctrl+F Object Snap, Ctrl+J Enter and the rest), and Settings' Edit shortcut keys… (or Keyboard Shortcuts in Ctrl+K) gives any command or action your own keys, shows what a key is assigned to before taking it, and keeps them on this device. Keys a browser tab keeps for itself are marked as working in the installed app.
