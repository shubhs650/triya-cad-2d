- M5-20 (#582): after a zoom, the selection preview and the rollover tooltip no longer show an object the resting cursor is not over. The canvas hands its render worker a new view only with its next frame, and a frame with raster images can take a while, so a cursor that came onto the canvas just after a typed ZOOM was tested under the old view: an image lit up and was named by a Raster Image tooltip over empty space (seen on CI in `image-attach.spec.ts`). The canvas now reads the pickbox and the cursor's rest again as the view reaches the worker, so both follow what is under the cursor in the new view; a test holds the worker's frames to make the view land late every run.
