- M2-17 part 1 (#251): Plot to PDF. PLOT, PRINT, Ctrl+P and the Plot… button open the Plot dialog: choose the paper (ISO A0–A4, ANSI A–E or a layout's own), what to plot (Layout, Extents, Display, Limits), the offset or Center the plot, Fit to paper or a scale, a plot style table (monochrome, grayscale or default CTB), lineweight options and the orientation, and OK downloads a vector PDF of the model or layout shown. Blocks plot through the plot style table as AutoCAD does (ByBlock and layer-0 parts take the reference's colour), layouts plot each viewport clipped to its frame, and objects on non-plotting layers such as DEFPOINTS are left out.
