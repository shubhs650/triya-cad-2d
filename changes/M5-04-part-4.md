- M5-04 part 4 (#514): the image commands: IMAGECLIP (ICL) clips an image to a rectangle, polygon or polyline (Invert clip hides the inside), turns its clip ON or OFF or deletes it; IMAGEADJUST and -IMAGEADJUST (IAD) set brightness, contrast and fade; IMAGEQUALITY sets High or Draft; IMAGEFRAME 0, 1 or 2 shows or hides image frames, and 2 ("displayed but not plotted") now survives a save and stays off plots. Each is one undo step and saves to DXF as AutoCAD keeps it.
