- M4-04 part 2 (#442): MLEADERSTYLE opens AutoCAD's Multileader Style Manager: make, modify, rename, delete and set multileader styles, with the Leader Format, Leader Structure and Content tabs (leader type, colour, linetype, arrowhead, landing, scale, text or block content, attachments, AutoCAD's built-in tag and callout blocks). Multileaders redraw when their style changes, and every setting survives a DXF save.
