- M6-02 part 11 (#611): VPORTS opens the Viewports dialog. New Viewports arranges model space's tiled viewports from the standard configurations (Display or Current Viewport, a New name saving it, each viewport's view chosen in the preview) or, in a layout, makes them as layout viewports with Viewport Spacing in an area picked after OK; Named Viewports shows the current configuration, renames and deletes configurations and restores or lays out the one picked. One undo step.
