- M9-02 part 1 (#649): `ConstraintModel` maps lines, circles, arcs, points and polyline segments to the PlaneGCS solver and applies AutoCAD's geometric constraints (all but Smooth, which waits for splines): the second object adjusts to the first, an edit re-solves the geometry constrained to it, and a constraint that would over-constrain the geometry is refused with the constraints in the way named. Degrees of freedom, conflicting and redundant constraints are reported for a loaded set. The solver module now redoes its diagnosis whenever the values it may change differ from the last solve.
