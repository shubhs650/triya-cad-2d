- M3-12 part 6 (#421): command preview (COMMANDPREVIEW, Options > Selection > Command preview). TRIM and EXTEND fade the part a pick would trim and show the extension, FILLET and CHAMFER show the corner as the cursor hovers over the second object, LENGTHEN shows the change and DYnamic's end, BREAK shows the gap, and OFFSET shows the copy on the cursor's side. TRIM's and EXTEND's Quick mode takes a freehand fence: press on nothing, drag across objects, let go.
