- M2-03 (#225): selection. Click an object to select it, drag or click a window left to right to take what lies inside it or right to left to take what it crosses, Shift+click to take objects out, Ctrl+A for everything and Esc to deselect; selected objects glow with a dashed outline. SELECT runs "Select objects" with AutoCAD's modes (Window, Crossing, BOX, ALL, Last, Previous, Add, Remove, Undo, AUto, SIngle) and its "n found, m total" lines, commands take what was selected first, and ZOOM Object zooms to the objects selected.
