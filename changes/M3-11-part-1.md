- M3-11 part 1 (#383): associative arrays. ARRAY, ARRAYRECT, ARRAYPOLAR and ARRAYPATH make one array object when ARRAYASSOCIATIVITY is 1 (initially, as in AutoCAD; the ASsociative option sets it), saved as AutoCAD saves an array's blocks with its definition in a `TRIYA_ARRAY` XRECORD (DEC-059, closes #379), and ARRAYEDIT changes its rows, columns, items, angles, method, alignment and base point after a reopen. Arrays made in AutoCAD stay untouched and are not editable.
