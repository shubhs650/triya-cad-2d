- M5-04 part 6 (#518): XATTACH (XA, or Attach DWG... in the References palette) attaches or overlays another drawing as an external reference: the Attach External Reference dialog picks the file (Browse...) or one of the drawing's xrefs, shows its Block Unit and the factor its references are scaled by, and places the reference by insertion point, X/Y/Z or uniform scale and rotation, typed in the dialog or given on screen. The drawing loads at once (with the xrefs it attaches itself), the References palette lists it, the save writes the new xref and its references to DXF (never its content), and U takes the xref, its content and its reference back in one step.
