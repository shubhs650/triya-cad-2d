- M2-22 (#249): Object snaps work on PDF underlays. Endpoint, Midpoint, Intersection, Center, Nearest and the other running snaps find the lines, arcs and curves of an attached PDF page, through the underlay's scale, rotation and clip (nothing outside the clip is snapped to). "Snap to PDF underlays" (PDFOSNAP) in Settings > Drafting turns it off; it is on by default and kept between sessions.
