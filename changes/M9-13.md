- M9-13 (#680): an associative array of constrained objects, and a block BLOCK makes of them, now keeps their constraints in the block definition instead of removing them. They are held there until ARRAYEDIT Source opens an item or the Block Editor shows the block, where edits re-solve them (MOVE of one line of a constrained L drags the other, and every item follows); ARRAYCLOSE keeps or discards them with the edit, a copy of the array gets its own, and they survive a save and reopen (DEC-087).
