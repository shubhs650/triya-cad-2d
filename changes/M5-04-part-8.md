- M5-04 part 8 (#522): PDFATTACH (or Attach PDF... in the References palette) attaches pages of a PDF as underlays: the Attach PDF Underlay dialog picks the file (Browse...) or one the drawing shows, lists its pages with their sizes for one or more to be selected, and takes the insertion point, scale and rotation or asks them at the command line; each page lands at its true size times the scale (several side by side), draws at once, is saved to DXF with a definition per page, and is undone in one step.
