- M2-27 part 1 (#269): more of the Enhanced Attribute Editor. Justification can be changed; the attribute's insertion point stays put and the text moves around it, as AutoCAD's Properties palette does (a multiline attribute offers the nine MTEXT attachments). Plot style lists the drawing's named plot styles, and Annotative shows whether the attribute is annotative. Double-clicking an attribute's text, or picking a block by one of its attributes, opens the editor on that attribute. Justified attributes save to DXF and read back.
