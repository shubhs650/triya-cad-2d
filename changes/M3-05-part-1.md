- M3-05 part 1 (#391): multi-functional grips: a polyline's vertex adds, removes or extends a vertex, a segment's midpoint adds a vertex or turns the segment into an arc (or an arc back into a line), a line's or arc's end lengthens it and an arc's midpoint sets its radius; a tap of Ctrl at the hot grip goes on to its next function, functions and the grip mode names (ST, MO, RO, SC, MI) can be typed, and the result follows the cursor.
