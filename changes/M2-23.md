- M2-23 (#252): Undo works in the app. Ctrl+Z undoes the last command and Ctrl+Y redoes it (Cmd+Shift+Z on a Mac), the title bar has Undo and Redo buttons named by the step they would take back ("Undo LINE"), and each undo shows a short toast with what it did and how many steps are left. UNDO, U, REDO and OOPS run at the command line, and freezing, locking or turning off a layer in the Layers palette, or renaming a layout, is a step of its own that U undoes.
