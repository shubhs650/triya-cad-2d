- M7-06 part 1 (#634): AutoCAD's whole default keyboard map. New keys: Ctrl+0 Clean Screen (with CLEANSCREENON/OFF and a status bar button), Ctrl+Shift+H to hide and show the palettes (HIDEPALETTES/SHOWPALETTES), Ctrl+F2 command history (TEXTSCR/GRAPHSCR), Ctrl+R next viewport, Ctrl+Page Up/Down previous and next layout, Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+F6 next drawing, Ctrl+[ and Ctrl+\ as Esc, and Ctrl+Q QUIT (EXIT) closing every drawing. Every AutoCAD default key Triya leaves out is listed with its reason in the keymap tests.
