- M2-07 part 1 (#230): TEXT (DTEXT) and MTEXT, typed in place on the canvas. TEXT takes every justification and places one line after another; MTEXT opens an editor on the box you drag, with a Text Formatting toolbar (bold, italic, underline, overline, strikethrough, height, colour, stacked fractions and tolerances). The formatting is saved as AutoCAD's MTEXT format codes and reads back exactly from the saved DXF.
